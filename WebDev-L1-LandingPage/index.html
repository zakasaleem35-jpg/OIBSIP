Our story

From a roadside dhaba to your kitchen

Daastan Chai started with one question: why does chai from a roadside dhaba taste better than chai made at home? The answer was fresh spices, patience and the right leaves.

We now bottle that ritual into three blends — Classic Doodh Patti, Elaichi Royale and Kashmiri Pink — each with a brewing card so anyone can make a proper cup.
